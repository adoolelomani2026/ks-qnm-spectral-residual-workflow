# Cover letter

21 July 2026

Dear Editors of *Classical and Quantum Gravity*,

Please find enclosed the major revision of manuscript CQG-116364, now entitled
*Low-lying quasinormal modes of Kazakov--Solodukhin black holes from Chebyshev
and continued-fraction methods*.

The revision addresses every referee comment and substantially strengthens both
the physical formulation and the numerical audit. We derive the scalar radial
equation explicitly; formulate the odd-parity problem with a gauge-invariant
sourced master equation under a clearly stated inverse-Cowling closure; and
regenerate all axial results with that effective-source potential. We also give
the full candidate-selection algorithm and Schwarzschild seed provenance,
continued-fraction-depth and Taylor-order convergence studies, polynomial
backward errors, potential and stability audits, and comparison with a pinned
external high-precision calculation.

The revised claims are deliberately limited to the evidence. The minimally
coupled scalar fundamentals are the principal physical result; first overtones
are reported as cross-validated low-lying modes; second overtones are
exploratory diagnostics; and the axial frequencies remain conditional on the
inverse-Cowling closure. The manuscript does not claim a general high-overtone
solver or a unique nonspherical quantum completion of the KS source.

All axial frequencies, figures, tables, shifts, stability diagnostics, and
external comparisons in the revised manuscript were regenerated using the
inverse-Cowling effective-source potential. No accepted numerical results from
the earlier lapse-substitution ansatz remain.

A clean manuscript, a marked line-numbered manuscript, a point-by-point
response, the completed revision checklist, and the updated source archive are
included. Thank you for considering the revised work.
